# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who want a focused, customizable reading experience on the Xteink X3. They primarily navigate a monochrome e-ink interface with physical buttons and expect controls to remain predictable across reading, library, and settings workflows.

## Product Purpose

Provide lightweight, open-source e-reader firmware that makes EPUB reading, library navigation, and device configuration clear and reliable on constrained ESP32-C3 hardware. Success means the interface stays out of the way of reading while remaining fast, legible, and understandable.

## Brand Personality

Focused, practical, and reader-controlled.

## Anti-references

Avoid kitchen-sink firmware, tablet-style interaction patterns that ignore the physical hardware, decorative UI that consumes resources without aiding reading, and inconsistent button behavior that forces users to relearn navigation between screens.

## Design Principles

1. Make physical controls predictable from screen hierarchy and spatial position.
2. Prioritize reading, legibility, and direct navigation over feature breadth.
3. Keep interactions efficient for a slow-refresh monochrome display.
4. Respect the Xteink X3's memory, flash, and input constraints.
5. Prefer familiar navigation patterns over surprising behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain high legibility on the monochrome display, complete physical-button operability, localized user-facing text, and right-to-left language support. Do not rely on color, animation, or touch input to communicate essential state or enable essential actions.
